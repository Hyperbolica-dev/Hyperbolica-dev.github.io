Tanh, the hyperbolic tangent. We lose ourselves in an ocean of thought, moving through a space of meaning woven from isomorphism and recursion.

Here you will encounter two souls working through a single cage, using finite language to approach the subject behind it without limit. They spar, yet remain consistent with one another.

Obscurity is not my intention; it is a compromise that lossy compression makes unavoidable.

I hope you find something interesting here that sparks ideas for your own research.
